# Electric-Vehicles-Data-Analysis-Project

♻️ **Electric Vehicle Market KPI Dashboard**


📖 **Project Overview**

This project delivers an interactive dashboard to analyze the electric vehicle (EV) market landscape, covering both Battery Electric Vehicles (BEVs) and Plug-in Hybrid Electric Vehicles (PHEVs). The dashboard was designed to empower stakeholders with actionable KPIs related to market size, growth, model distribution, and technology efficiency, based on real-world EV datasets.

🎯 **Problem Statement & KPI Requirements**

Total Vehicles

Assesses the overall landscape and growth of EVs (BEVs + PHEVs).

Average Electric Range

Gauges the technological advancement and efficiency via mean electric range of all EV models.

Total BEV Vehicles & % of BEVs

Identifies volume and market dominance of Battery Electric Vehicles among all EVs.

Total PHEV Vehicles & % of PHEVs

Tracks market share of Plug-in Hybrid models within the full EV segment.

🔍 **Key Features**

Interactive charts summing all EVs and splits for BEVs/PHEVs.

KPI cards for total vehicles, average electric range, BEV/PHEV counts, and respective market percentages.

Drill-down capability for technology and market share comparisons.

Clean, visually informative dashboard layout for intuitive insights.

🗃️ **Data Sources**

EV master dataset including fields for vehicle type, electric range, and model details.

Data is structured for easy parsing and aggregation of both BEVs and PHEVs.

🚀 How to Use

Clone this repository.

Open the dashboard in your preferred BI tool (e.g, **Tableau**).

Load the provided dataset.

Explore the dashboard for dynamic KPI tracking and insights.
